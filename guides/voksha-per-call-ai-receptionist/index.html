Voksha: the $14 AI Receptionist — Per-Call Pricing, a Security Pitch, and a Booking Catch

Voksha prices its AI receptionist per call, not per minute: $14/month for 15 calls, $99 for 150, $1 per extra call, with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance claimed. The catch: appointment booking and integrations start only on the $99 Premium plan.

By Jason O’Keefe·October 31, 2026

How we checked this guide: every Voksha price, call allowance, and feature below was read from Voksha’s own pricing page (voksha.com/pricing) on November 1, 2026. The Goodcall and Frontdesk figures are the vendor-verified prices already published in our cost guide. Disclosure: this site is run by the founder of OplyAI, an AI front-desk company. This page contains affiliate links; see our affiliate disclosure.

Per-minute billing punishes you for the calls that matter most. Per-caller billing ignores how long they last. Voksha bills a third way: per answered call.

The pricing: $14, $99, or $990+

Voksha’s own pricing page lists three plans, all month-to-month with no contracts and no setup fee:

Every plan includes 24/7 answering, lead capture, call summaries, email and SMS alerts, phone scam protection, and encrypted calls (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit). Voksha claims SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance on every plan — a vendor claim, stated as such, and the distinct angle in its pitch: the cheapest per-call entrant with a published security story. Spam and robocalls Voksha screens out do not count against your plan, and you get alerts at 75% and 90% of your included calls.

The math that decides the plan

Voksha’s own FAQ does this math for you, and it checks out: at 100 calls a month, Starter costs $14 + 85 × $1 = $99 — exactly the Premium price. So the two plans converge at 100 calls, and the real Premium value is not price but features: appointment booking and integrations start only on Premium (79 tools, 6 self-serve), along with after-hours routing, custom call flows, and priority support. At 30 calls a month, Starter costs $14 + 15 = $29.

The pattern: Voksha wins on paper for low-volume shops that want a security story; Goodcall wins once volume or call length grows, because its minutes are unlimited and its caller cap is generous. And a shop that needs booking must budget $99 on Voksha regardless — the $14 tier answers and captures, it does not book.

The catch, stated plainly

Two things buyers routinely miss on the $14 headline:

  1. No booking on Starter. Appointment booking, integrations, after-hours routing, and custom call flows all start on Premium. If your shop’s bottleneck is scheduling, $14 does not solve it.
  2. No free trial. Instead of a trial, Voksha offers a free demo call (1-888-488-1811) and a 7-day money-back guarantee on every plan. Hear it before you pay — the demo line exists for exactly that.

Voksha’s August 2026 press release claims 1 million+ calls handled since its December 2025 launch — a vendor claim, not an independent count, and we are labeling it as such rather than repeating it as fact. Its pricing page also states it is trusted by 1,200+ local businesses in the US, Canada, and the UK — again, the vendor’s own statement.

The bottom line

Voksha is the cheapest honest entry point in AI answering for a low-volume shop — $14 for 15 calls, $1 a call after, with a security pitch no other budget entrant publishes. But the $14 tier is answering-only: if you need booking, budget $99, and if your calls run long or your volume grows past 100 a month, Goodcall Starter at $79 with unlimited minutes Try Goodcall is still the plan to beat. Model your peak month in calls, not minutes, and call the demo line before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Voksha cost?

Voksha's own pricing page, verified November 1, 2026, lists Starter at $14 per month for 15 included calls, Premium at $99 per month for 150 included calls, and Enterprise from $990 per month (custom). Every additional call on Starter or Premium costs $1. There are no per-minute charges, no contracts, and no setup fee.

Does the $14 Voksha plan book appointments?

No. Appointment booking and integrations start on the $99 Premium plan, per Voksha's own pricing table. The $14 Starter answers 24/7, captures leads, and sends call summaries — it does not book. A shop that needs booking must budget $99, not $14.

How does per-call pricing compare with per-minute and per-caller plans?

It is the third billing unit in this market. Goodcall bills per unique caller ($79 for 100 callers, unlimited minutes); Frontdesk bills per minute ($99 for 200 voice minutes); Voksha bills per answered call ($14 for 15, $99 for 150, $1 each after). Short calls favor per-call billing; long calls favor per-caller plans where minutes do not matter.

Is there a free trial?

No free trial. Voksha offers a free demo call (1-888-488-1811, published on its pricing page) and a 7-day money-back guarantee on every plan instead.
