Which AI receptionist should answer your phones?

An AI receptionist is software that answers your business phone line itself — it holds a real conversation, figures out what the caller needs, books the appointment, and transfers urgent calls to you. No hold music, no voicemail, no missed jobs at 9pm. This site exists to tell you which services do that well, what they actually cost, and which one fits your trade.
