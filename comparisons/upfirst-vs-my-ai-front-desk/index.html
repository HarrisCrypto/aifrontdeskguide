Upfirst vs. My AI Front Desk (Frontdesk): Which AI Receptionist Should You Buy?

Upfirst vs My AI Front Desk compared on verified 2026 pricing: Upfirst from $24.95/mo per-call billing vs Frontdesk $99/mo multi-channel credits. Cost scenarios, features, and who should pick which.

By Jason O’Keefe·October 12, 2026·Updated October 17, 2026

How we checked this comparison: all plans, prices, and feature claims were read from upfirst.ai and myaifrontdesk.com on October 13, 2026; Frontdesk’s free-plan removal was re-verified on its pricing page on October 18, 2026. This page contains affiliate links; see our affiliate disclosure.

The short answer

Upfirst and My AI Front Desk (now branded Frontdesk) solve the same problem — nobody misses your calls — with opposite philosophies. Upfirst is a phone-first, per-call receptionist starting at $24.95/month: cheap, simple, and you pay per answered call. Frontdesk is a multi-channel front office at $99/month: the AI receptionist plus web chat, SMS, forms, CRM, and automations, metered by usage credits. Pick Upfirst if the phone is the job; pick Frontdesk if you want the whole front desk — phone, chat, text, and follow-up — in one subscription.

Feature differences that actually matter

Where Upfirst is stronger:

  • Per-call billing favors real-world phone behavior. A 12-minute intake and a 1-minute message cost the same. On Frontdesk’s per-minute credits, long calls eat your allowance.
  • Spam economics. Upfirst’s never-billed-for-spam policy is stated outright. If your number is on every robocall list in the county, this is real money.
  • Language coverage. 30+ languages with automatic switching is published on their site — relevant if your customer base is multilingual.
  • Simplicity. One product, four plans, one bill. Nothing to configure beyond the phone agent.

Where Frontdesk is stronger:

  • It’s not just a phone. Web chatbot, SMS agent, website forms, CRM, and follow-up sequences are all in the $99. Replacing those separately would cost more than the difference.
  • Outbound. 20 AI-placed outbound calls per day included — appointment reminders, follow-ups — where Upfirst is inbound-focused.
  • No feature gates. Zapier, verified outbound number, and the full automation suite come with Business-in-a-Box; you’re never upsold to unlock something.

Where neither wins: neither publishes a human-backup option (compare Smith.ai’s live-agent transfer if that matters), and neither publishes HIPAA/BAA terms on the pricing pages we checked — get compliance in writing before routing regulated calls through either.

Verdict: pick Upfirst if… / pick Frontdesk if…

Pick Upfirst if: the phone is the job — you want missed calls answered, messages taken, appointments booked, and summaries texted to you, at the lowest cost per call. Solo operators, trades, and anyone whose website already handles its own chat should start here. The 14-day trial with no credit card makes it a free experiment.

Pick Frontdesk if: you want one subscription to cover the phone and the website chat and text follow-up and a basic CRM — and you’d rather pay $99 for the bundle than stitch together three tools. Businesses doing meaningful web-chat or SMS volume get the most out of it.

Pick neither yet if: you need a human to catch the hard calls (look at hybrid options), or your call volume is under ~25 calls a month (Smith.ai’s free tier covers that at $0).

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Upfirst or My AI Front Desk?

Upfirst is cheaper at low call volumes: $24.95/month for 30 calls vs Frontdesk’s $99/month for 200 voice minutes. At around 100 calls a month, Upfirst’s Premium plan ($59.95 for 90 calls plus overage) lands near $70 while Frontdesk lands near $124 — but Frontdesk bundles web chat, SMS, and a CRM that Upfirst doesn’t. Compare on your volume, not the sticker price.

Does My AI Front Desk still exist? Is it called Frontdesk now?

Yes — the company now brands itself as Frontdesk (myaifrontdesk.com), though many buyers still search for “My AI Front Desk.” Same product: an AI receptionist bundled with a web chatbot, SMS agent, forms, CRM, and automations.

Does Upfirst charge for spam calls?

No. Upfirst states on its own site that spam calls, robocalls, quick hang-ups, and calls under 15 seconds are never billed — they’re filtered automatically.

Which one is better for appointment booking?

Both book appointments: Upfirst connects to Google Calendar, Outlook, Clio, Acuity, and Calendly; Frontdesk includes scheduling plus SMS follow-up and a built-in CRM. If booking plus automated follow-up sequences matter, Frontdesk’s bundle is stronger. If you just need the phone answered and booked, Upfirst is simpler and cheaper.

Can I try either one free?

Upfirst offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Frontdesk does not offer a permanently free plan — the entry point is the paid Business-in-a-Box plan ($99/month, or $79/month billed annually). So if you want a no-cost test drive, Upfirst is the only one of the two that offers it. Verified October 18, 2026.
